## T2 - which structure do real fixes follow? (cross-validated by bug)

**57 one-token substitutions** between project-independent tokens (operators, literals, keywords), from BugsInPy 12, Defects4J 29, Defects4C 16 - out of 1729 real bugs. 10-fold cross-validation by bug: each fold's substitutions are held out once, the 16-token vocabulary, the placement and the T classes are fitted on the other folds alone. **This sample is small - read T2 as a direction, not a verdict.**

**51%** of real substitutions are flips: the reverse substitution is also a real fix somewhere in the corpus.
