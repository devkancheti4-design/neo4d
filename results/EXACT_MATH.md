## 2. How many worked examples pin a whole relabelling of the 16 kinds

| structure | relabellings | examples that pin one | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| ring of 16 (static fluidnet) | 16 | **1** | one example fixes the shift |

neo4d covers 20,160x the ring's relabellings for 4 more examples, and needs **1/3** of the examples a structure-free learner needs for a full relabelling (5 vs 15).

## 4. A truth outside the structure - a random permutation of the 16 codes

Five examples with affinely independent cases, query uniform over the 16 codes. An affine map fits exactly when the four action differences are independent: (12 x 8) / (13 x 12) = **8/13**. If it fits, the router answers - right on the 5 example cases, and on each other case right only if the permutation happens to agree (1 in 11). If it does not fit, it refuses everything.

| | exact | = | fresh simulation, 200,000 | measured before (`results/ROBUSTNESS.md`, 20,000) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| right | **3/13** | 23.0769% | 23.0930% | 22.99% |
| refused | **5/13** | 38.4615% | 38.2970% | 38.44% |
| wrong | **5/13** | 38.4615% | 38.6100% | 38.57% |

Exact for a truth inside the structure: wrong = **0** (sections 1 and the judges). Outside it, the 5/13 is the price of answering when five points happen to fit - stated, not hidden.

## 5. A ghost query - examples spanning rank 3, the query outside the span

8 affine maps survive and give 8 different answers, each equally likely when the truth is uniform over the class. Any rule that answers is right with probability at most **1/8 = 12.5%**; neo4d refuses, so it is wrong with probability **0**. Measured (`results/ADVERSARIAL.md`): neo4d refused 100%, a nearest-example heuristic was right 6.5% (below the 1/8 ceiling - its guess is not always one of the 8).

## 6. The verification cost of learning one whole relabelling

Each worked example costs 4 test-suite runs (caught, restored, and both re-checked - measured, `results/HARVEST_PICK.md`):

| learner | examples | suite runs | relative |
|---|---|---|---|
| neo4d (affine, span-aware) | 5 | **20** | 1 |
| structure-free (any permutation) | 15 | 60 | 3x |
| test every kind directly | 16 | 64 | 16/5x |

Measured on click: **20** suite runs, both secret renumberings. One router decision after that: ~4 ns (`results/SILICON.md`); one click suite run: ~3.7 s - the router is not where the time goes; the examples are, and the structure cuts them to a third.
