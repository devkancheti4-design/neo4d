## E4 - a noisy teacher (10,000 lessons, a share of them wrong)

A wrong lesson: one random token of the line replaced by a random token of the corpus, presented as a mutant. Tested against the true teacher on held-out modules.

| 30% | **96.1%** | **1.1%** | 95.9% / **27.3%** | 96.1% / 0.6% |
| 50% | **95.5%** | **0.9%** | 95.4% / **20.0%** | 95.5% / 0.5% |

The law spares a cheap relation from weakening (R9: "unless cheap or proven"), so every wrong lesson that makes a cheap rule stays active, and a thousand of them spray. The principle test drops that one exemption - the table it allows, built here only to measure the change, not handed over as a law.
