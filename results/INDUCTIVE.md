Expected examples, passive: **215/39** = 5.5128 (simulated 5.5115); active: **5**, always. Five is the minimum any learner can achieve here - pinning M needs four independent differences, so five points. In test runs (4 per example): active 20, passive 22.05 on average. Span alone is a modest saving in 4D; PICK's larger measured win (79 and 39 test runs down to 20) came from skipping trials the suite cannot catch or several edits would repair.
