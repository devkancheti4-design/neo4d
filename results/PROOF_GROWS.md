## What this proves, and what it does not

- It mutates: on code it never saw it generates real mutants, checked one by one against an independent teacher.
- Its knowledge grows: more teaching gives more active patterns and more coverage of unseen code; on real bugs, later fixes are covered more often than earlier ones, by a margin a significance test rejects as chance.
- It does not prove the growth continues without limit: in the closed domain it levels off near its ceiling, and on real bugs it is limited to edits of up to three tokens.
