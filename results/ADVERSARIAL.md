# Ghost-space queries - neo4d against a guessing heuristic

Generated by `neo4d/adversarial.py`. 20,000 scenarios: four harvested examples spanning a rank-3 subspace, one query just outside it.

- every ghost query is one bit from a span element: 20,000/20,000
- answers still open per ghost query (brute force over 322,560 maps): {8: 20000}

| | right | refused | **wrong** |
|---|---|---|---|
| neo4d (route4d5) | 0.0% | 100.0% | **0.0%** |
| nearest-example heuristic | 6.5% | 0.0% | **93.5%** |

With 8 answers equally open, any guess is right 1 time in 8 at best: the heuristic's right-rate is the price of guessing, not bad luck. neo4d's refusal is not a probability - the Determined Test fails, so it never answers.
